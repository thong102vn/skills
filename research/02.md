## Coverage-Driven Decomposition (CDD)

  - OPEN-WORLD EVIDENCE: Design from variation axes, not customer files. Customer data is optional, but every support claim requires independent specification, generated, public-sample, or
  consented and redacted evidence.
  - SUPPORT SCOPE: Claim support only for an explicit input class defined by container and packaging, producer/version, dataset topology, semantic grain, locale/encoding, security properties, and
  target domain; never claim support by file extension alone.
  - INTAKE GATE: Treat one intake as 1..N immutable artifacts and each artifact as 0..N datasets. Prove identity, scope, grain, authority, completeness, duplication, and overlap before combining or
  committing them.
  - LINEAGE GATE: Every normalized fact retains artifact hash, dataset and region location, raw value, formula and cached value where present, parser version, mapping version, transformations, and
  validation results.
  - TRUTH GATE: Source shape is dynamic, but canonical identity, grain, money, temporal, balancing, and integrity contracts are strict. Uncertainty remains staged and cannot silently become
  accounting truth.
  - SAFETY GATE: Validate MIME and signature, encryption, macros, external links, decompression, size, malware, PII access, residency, and retention before parsing or exposing content.
  - UNKNOWN-INPUT GATE: Every dataset resolves to verified auto-import, guided mapping, evidence-only retention, or rejection with an exact repair path. Unknown values are never guessed.
  - SUPPORT GATE: An input class is supported only when intake, discovery, classification, mapping, domain admission, idempotent commit, receipt, recovery, and downstream-reader paths are
  executable and tested.
  - TEST GATE: Use golden, generated, mutation, property, and metamorphic fixtures across topology, grain, authority, identity, time, locale, formulas, overlap, corruption, security, and scale.
  - CLAIM GATE: An enum, table, title, sample mapping, successful upload, or feature pointer proves presence only. Support requires named executable evidence and verified delivery status.

  0. Discover and challenge the axes before enumerating their values. Apply open discovery recursively: axes -> values -> cells. Ask where variation exists across actor, lifecycle, obligation,
  legal state, jurisdiction tier, channel, source topology, authority, grain, data availability, time, scale, and failure mode. Use an independent lens to challenge the axis set.

  1. Discover every axis's values until second-source-dry: stop only when a fresh independent source adds no new value. Do not test saturation only on dominant or familiar values.

  2. Cross the 2-3 highest-variance axes into the primary matrix. Every un-crossed axis must be an explicit overlay sweep against the full epic set. An axis is either crossed or swept; it is never
  silently excluded.

  3. Every cell must record: cell ID, conditions, evidence source, confidence, impact, frequency, severity, disposition, delivery status (`designed`, `implemented`, or `verified`), covering epic
  and feature IDs, executable contract and test IDs, downstream reader, recovery path, and re-entry trigger. A pointer without executable evidence remains an unverified claim.

  4. Decide reuse, extend, buy, or build when each cell is created. Record OSS candidates and assess license, maintenance, security, extensibility, data-residency, operational, and lock-in fit
  before selecting a disposition.

  5. Run independent critics across multiple lenses, including persona, jurisdiction, lifecycle, operations, accessibility, privacy, security, fraud, accounting integrity, and failure recovery.
  Critics must attack omissions, weak coverage, incorrect dispositions, unsupported claims, and broken edges between features.

  6. Loop critic findings back into axis discovery and rerun the affected matrix and overlays. Stop only at double saturation: discovery is second-source-dry and at least two consecutive full
  critic passes (`K >= 2`) find no material new issue.

  7. Publish coverage and known gaps using: `covered X (evidence E, verification V); deferred Y (owner O, reason R, re-entry trigger Z, review date D)`. A deferral without an owner and trigger is
  an omission.

  - DEPTH DIAL: Scale discovery breadth, matrix dimensionality, evidence volume, and critic-lens count to the stakes. Never reduce truth, lineage, safety, unknown-input handling, or support gates.

  ## UI/UX Review

  1. Walk complete paths, not references. For each actor, identify what they physically do, what returns, what happens next, who reads it, and how they recover. A step without a next step is a
  defect.
  2. Name the reader and required action for every output, event, notification, export, and audit record. An output with no consumer is a leak.
  3. Expand every noun phrase into exact content, version, source, timing, permissions, states, and actions. Anything that cannot be rendered or implemented is a stub.
  4. Apply the ordering test to every precondition: prove that the dependency exists, is current, and is authorized at that exact point in the workflow.
  5. When adding or changing a rule, enumerate every obligation it creates across contracts, schemas, workflows, permissions, wireframes, telemetry, tests, help content, and operations.
  6. Physically simulate every human interaction: upload, scan, photograph, paste, type, select, map, correct, approve, undo, retry, read, hear, dictate, export, and hand off.
  7. Review composed journeys after individual components. Verify that filters, selection, pagination, bulk actions, totals, permissions, drafts, audit history, and cross-surface navigation remain
  coherent together.
  8. Cover loading, empty, partial, stale, duplicate, conflicting, unauthorized, offline, timeout, validation-error, processing, success, rollback, and unrecoverable states.
  9. Verify responsive layout, keyboard operation, focus order, screen-reader semantics, contrast, locale formatting, long Vietnamese text, large datasets, and production performance.
  10. Run a final adversarial pass whose sole purpose is to break the completed journey, invalidate its evidence, and expose unsupported edge conditions.
