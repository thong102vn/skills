## Coverage-Driven Decomposition (CDD)

  - BASELINE: Before reading the design, inventory capabilities from one enterprise product, one SME product, one jurisdiction source and one practitioner journey.
  - OPEN WORLD: Design from variation classes, not customer samples; samples are optional regression evidence, never the support boundary.
  - SCOPE: State target segments, operating models, jurisdictions, channels and explicit assisted/deferred boundaries before decomposition.
  - AXES: Discover variation across actor, segment, business model, input, cardinality, composition, nesting, destination, evidence, lifecycle, time, jurisdiction, authority, scale, device,
    accessibility and failure.

  - OUTCOMES: Treat detect → explain → decide → execute/post → consume → reverse/recover → measure as one lifecycle; a decision is never a terminal edge.
  - FLOWS: Cross gross/net, aggregation, split, partial, fee, refund, chargeback, withholding, FX, delay and one/multiple destinations where money or inventory moves.
  - SATURATION: Discover every axis and value until two fresh independent sources add nothing; rerun discovery whenever a critic exposes a missing value.
  - MATRIX: Cross the 2–3 highest-variance axes; sweep every remaining axis across the complete feature set, never silently excluding one.
  - CELLS: Classify every meaningful cell as proved, assisted fallback, ambiguous or missing; record evidence, confidence, severity, owner and feature/test IDs.
  - EDGE GATE: For every cell trace precondition/surface → API/schema/IAM → normalization/evaluation → explanation/action → execution/posting → downstream reader → reversal/recovery →
    measurement → release test.

  - INPUT GATE: When external input exists, prove immutable intake → dataset discovery → grain/authority → mapping → admission → idempotent commit → receipt/recovery.
  - TRUTH GATE: Keep source shape dynamic but canonical identity, money, time, balance and integrity strict; uncertainty remains staged.
  - LINEAGE/SAFETY: Retain source location, raw value, transformations, versions and hashes; validate type, security, corruption, privacy, residency and retention.
  - UNKNOWN GATE: Resolve every unknown to verified automation, guided handling, evidence-only retention or rejection with repair; never guess domain truth.
  - BOUNDARY SEARCH: Hunt singular/plural drift, closed unions, mixed-payload rejection, array-position inference, UI-only controls, assumed selectors and one-variant tests.
  - BENCHMARK DELTA: Convert every external capability into a covered, assisted, deferred or missing cell; references support discovery but never prove parity.
  - REUSE: Decide reuse, extend, buy or build per cell and record license, maintenance, security, extensibility, residency, operations and lock-in evidence.
  - SCENARIOS: Test the smallest normal, heterogeneous boundary and mixed/adversarial extension cases for every high-risk capability.
  - EXTENSION TEST: Add an unknown future adapter/type that must render generically or reach an explicit human fallback without a new core branch or page.
  - UX WALK: Physically simulate each actor from missing preconditions through input, response, next action, reader and recovery across desktop, mobile, keyboard, screen reader and degraded
    states.

  - COMPOSE REVIEW: Review complete journeys and cross-surface consequences after components; every output names its reader and every noun expands to exact content/version/timing.
  - CRITICS: Run independent persona, practitioner, jurisdiction, operations, accessibility, privacy, security, fraud and integrity critics against omissions, weak coverage, wrong
    dispositions and broken edges.

  - STOP: Stop only after axis discovery, benchmark deltas and independent journey critics are each dry for two consecutive full passes.
  - CLAIMS: Presence is not support; report DESIGNED, IMPLEMENTED and VERIFIED separately and name exact supported cardinalities/compositions plus unsupported cells.
  - DEFERRALS: Record deferred Y; owner O; reason R; trigger Z; review date D; a deferral without owner and trigger is missing.
  - HYGIENE: Rewrite the authoritative body and update all obligated artifacts; never append amendments, duplicate facts or create parallel gap documents.
  - DEPTH: Scale matrix breadth and critic count to risk, but never weaken truth, lineage, safety, edge, evidence or claim gates.

  ## UI/UX Review

  1. Walk complete paths, not references. For each actor, identify what they physically do, what returns, what happens next, who reads it, and how they recover. A step without a next step is a
  defect.
  2. Name the reader and required action for every output, event, notification, export, and audit record. An output with no consumer is a leak.
  3. Expand every noun phrase into exact content, version, source, timing, permissions, states, and actions. Anything that cannot be rendered or implemented is a stub.
  4. Apply the ordering test to every precondition: prove that the dependency exists, is current, and is authorized at that exact point in the workflow.
  5. When adding or changing a rule, enumerate every obligation it creates across contracts, schemas, workflows, permissions, wireframes, telemetry, tests, help content, and operations.
  6. Physically simulate every human interaction: upload, scan, photograph, paste, type, select, map, correct, approve, undo, retry, read, hear, dictate, export, and hand off.
  7. Review composed journeys after individual components. Verify that filters, selection, pagination, bulk actions, totals, permissions, drafts, audit history, and cross-surface navigation remain
  coherent together.
  8. Cover loading, empty, partial, stale, duplicate, conflicting, unauthorized, offline, timeout, validation-error, processing, success, rollback, and unrecoverable states.
  9. Verify responsive layout, keyboard operation, focus order, screen-reader semantics, contrast, locale formatting, long Vietnamese text, large datasets, and production performance.
  10. Run a final adversarial pass whose sole purpose is to break the completed journey, invalidate its evidence, and expose unsupported edge conditions.
